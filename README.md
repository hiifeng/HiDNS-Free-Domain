## 📖 About HiDNS
 
HiDNS operates as a **Private Domain Name Registry (PDNR)**, providing free subdomain registrations within the HiDNS namespace. The project was initiated by **ifeng in November 2024** as an independent effort to explore a more open and developer-friendly domain namespace.
 
The name **HiDNS** comes from "Hello DNS" — a simple greeting to developers, creators, and anyone building on the internet. What began as a small experiment has since evolved into a stable platform accepted into the **Public Suffix List (PSL)** in August 2025, enabling broad compatibility across browsers and internet services.
 
HiDNS is not backed by venture capital and does not operate as a commercial domain registrar. It is maintained as a long-term community infrastructure project focused on **reliability, careful governance, and responsible operation** of a shared namespace.
 
> *"The goal is not to become the largest free subdomain provider, but to remain a clean, trustworthy, and stable option for individuals and projects that genuinely need one."*

## ✨ What We Offer
 
- **100% free subdomains** — no registration fees, no renewal fees
- **Three available parent domains:**
  - `hidns.cc`
  - `hidns.co`
  - `hidns.vip`
- **Supported DNS record types:**
  - `A` / `AAAA` — point to an IPv4 or IPv6 address
  - `CNAME` — alias to another domain
  - `MX` — mail exchange records
  - `TXT` — text records for verification, SPF, DKIM, etc.
  - `NS` — delegate to your own nameservers
  - `DS` — DNSSEC delegation signer records
- **Long-term availability** — parent domains are actively maintained and renewed by HiDNS
- **Public Suffix List accepted** — full browser and service compatibility
- **Ideal for:**
  - Personal websites and portfolios
  - Open-source and self-hosted projects
  - Student, lab, and academic work
  - Small online communities
  - Learning, testing, and experimentation

## 🔐 Invite-Only Registration
 
HiDNS uses an **invitation-based registration model** to balance openness with responsible stewardship of the shared namespace. This is not meant to restrict access — it is a chain of trust designed to ensure the namespace remains healthy and abuse-free.
 
### How to Get an Invite Code
 
The process is similar to how V2EX works: **ask a friend who is already using HiDNS** for an invitation code. Invite codes are also periodically distributed through the [official Telegram channel](https://t.me/HiDNS_FreeDomain_Channel).
 
**Invite code eligibility** is granted to users who have used HiDNS normally for more than three years without abuse.
 
### Step-by-Step Registration
 
1. **Obtain an invite code** from an existing HiDNS user or via the official Telegram channel
2. **Visit [hidoha.net](https://www.hidoha.net)** and sign up using your invite code with a regularly used email address
3. **Choose a subdomain prefix** — it must be concise, appropriate, and relevant to your intended use
4. **Submit your domain application** through the control panel (no payment required)
5. **Open a support ticket** describing your intended use — your domain will be reviewed and activated within **72 hours**
6. **Follow the [Telegram channel](https://t.me/HiDNS_FreeDomain_Channel)** to stay updated on announcements and policies
 
> ⚠️ Please use a long-term, regularly checked email address. If HiDNS is unable to contact you regarding security issues, your domain may be suspended.
 
### Account & Domain Limits

## 🛡️ Abuse Prevention & Fair Use
 
Because this is a free shared namespace, abuse prevention is essential to keeping it available for everyone. HiDNS enforces the following:
 
- Manual review of domain applications and intended use statements
- Monitoring of subdomains, including those appearing in certificate transparency logs
- Detection of phishing, malware, spam, fraud, and other malicious activity
- Strict **one-person-one-account** policy
- Immediate suspension of domains involved in serious violations
 
**The following are strictly prohibited:**
 
- Phishing websites or fraudulent services
- Malware, trojans, virus distribution, or C2 control
- Spam distribution and scam activities
- Unauthorized mirroring or malicious proxying of third-party websites
- Distributing pirated content (software, movies, music, etc.)
- Temporary or disposable email services
- Using HiDNS subdomains to build third-party bulk subdomain distribution platforms
 
> Domains involved in serious violations **may be suspended without prior notice**. Violations such as phishing or malware distribution are **non-appealable**.

## 🔄 Domain Renewal
 
Each subdomain has a validity period. To keep your domain active:
 
- Renewal is **free** and can be completed through the account management panel
- Renewal reminders are sent via email **90 days before expiration**
- Domains not renewed within the expiration window are considered inactive and **released for others to register**
- Cancelled domain configuration and data **cannot be recovered**

## 🚨 Reporting Abuse
 
If you discover abuse within the HiDNS namespace (`hidns.cc`, `hidns.co`, `hidns.vip`), please report it to us. All reports are reviewed manually.
 
**When reporting, please include:**
- The full domain name involved
- The specific URL where the abuse occurs
- A brief description of the issue
- Any supporting evidence (screenshots, logs, timestamps)
 
📧 Abuse reports: [support@hidoha.net](mailto:support@hidoha.net) or visit [hidoha.net/report-abuse](https://www.hidoha.net/report-abuse)
 
> Submitting false or malicious abuse reports may result in your report being ignored.
